Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 13, 2025

You have the right to request deletion of all personal data VideoGrab holds about you. This page explains how to do that.

What data is deleted

Upon request we will permanently delete:

  • Your Instagram user ID and username
  • Your Telegram chat ID
  • Your access token
  • The timestamps associated with your account

Any temporarily stored media files (videos/audio) are deleted automatically within 1 hour regardless of a deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to nurullomahmud@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your Instagram username or the Instagram user ID associated with your account so we can locate your record.
  3. We will process your request within 7 days and confirm by email once completed.

Revoking app permissions on Instagram

You can also revoke VideoGrab's access directly through Instagram:

  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) → Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap Apps and websites.
  4. Find VideoGrab and tap Remove.

Revoking permissions stops future access. To ensure all stored data is removed, please also send us an email as described above.
