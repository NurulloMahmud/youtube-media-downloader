Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2025

VideoGrab ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal productivity tool that lets users download videos from YouTube and Instagram and receive them via Telegram. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. What data we collect

When you connect your Instagram account through our service, we collect:

  • Your Instagram user ID (a numeric identifier assigned by Meta)
  • Your Instagram username
  • Your Telegram chat ID (when you link your Telegram account)
  • The date your account was created and linked

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, payment information, or any content beyond what is necessary to deliver the service.

2. How we use your data

The data listed above is used solely to:

  • Link your Instagram account to your Telegram account
  • Deliver downloaded video and audio files to your Telegram
  • Send you your access token via Instagram DM

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.

3. Data storage

Account linking data (Instagram ID, Telegram chat ID, token) is stored in a private PostgreSQL database on our server. Downloaded media files are stored temporarily and deleted automatically after 1 hour.

4. Data retention

Your account record is kept as long as you use the service. You may request deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Third-party services

This app uses the Meta (Instagram) Graph API and the Telegram Bot API to provide its functionality. Their respective privacy policies apply to data processed through their platforms.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to request data deletion, contact us at nurullomahmud@gmail.com.
